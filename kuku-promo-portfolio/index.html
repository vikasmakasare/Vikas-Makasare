Promo Writing Samples

Original demonstration samples created for this portfolio to showcase short-form promo thinking, hooks and dramatic pacing.

Sample 01 • Romance / Mystery

Us Raat Ke Baad

HOOK: “Usne kaha tha — raat ke 12 baje ke baad mera phone mat uthana.”

VO: “Lekin jab uska call aaya… main khud ko rok nahi paaya.”

“Do minute ki woh call… meri poori zindagi badalne wali thi.”

END: “Kuch raaz phone par nahi milte. Unke liye raat ka intezaar karna padta hai.”
Sample 02 • Family / Emotion

Maa Ko Mat Batana

HOOK: “Papa ne jaate waqt sirf ek baat kahi — Maa ko kuch mat batana.”

VO: “Mujhe laga woh paise ki baat kar rahe hain.”

“Phir maine unki diary kholi… aur pehli baar samjha ki woh mujhse kya chhupa rahe the.”

END: “Kabhi-kabhi parents apne bachchon se sach nahi… apna dard chhupate hain.”
Sample 03 • Suspense / Microdrama

Last Seen 2:17 AM

HOOK: “Uska WhatsApp last seen 2:17 AM tha.”

“Problem sirf itni thi… ki woh 11 baje mar chuki thi.”

VO: “Police ke paas koi jawab nahi tha.”

“Lekin mere phone par uska message abhi bhi aa raha tha.”

END: “Agar woh mar chuki hai… toh mujhe message kaun kar raha hai?”
Sample 04 • Hindi-English / Youth

You Were Never Just a Friend

HOOK: “We said we were just friends.”

“Phir ek din she stopped replying.”

“No fight. No explanation. Nothing.”

VO: “And that's when I realised…”

“Some people don't leave when they say goodbye. They leave much earlier — the moment you stop being honest about what you feel.”

END: “Maybe you were never just a friend.”

30-Second Promo Thinking

0–3 sec

Interrupt

Start with a question, contradiction, emotional shock or unresolved situation.

3–10 sec

Context

Give only enough information to make the audience understand what is at stake.

10–23 sec

Escalate

Introduce conflict, emotion or new information that raises the stakes.

23–30 sec

Withhold

End on an unanswered question, reveal, emotional beat or cliffhanger.
